# SECTION 1: CAMPAIGN OVERVIEW & GOALS

## Primary Objectives

**Objective 1: Registrations**
- Target: 200 attendees (50 foursomes + dinner-only guests)
- Golf registration goal: 180 players (45 foursomes x $150 = $27,000 gross)
- Dinner-only goal: 20+ guests at $75 = $1,500+
- Milestone check-ins: 25% sold by April 15, 75% sold by June 1, sold out by June 15

**Objective 2: Sponsorships**
- Target: 8-12 total sponsors across tiered packages
- Revenue goal: $25,000-$40,000 from sponsorships
- Existing anchor: Morongo Band of Mission Indians (Triple Crown Sponsor)
- Outreach target: 25 prospects, convert 40%

**Objective 3: Auction & Donation Revenue**
- Silent auction goal: $10,000+
- Raffle revenue: $3,000+
- Spontaneous donations (during tribute/remarks): $2,000+

**Objective 4: Awareness & Legacy Building**
- Grow combined social following by 15-20% (10,000 to 11,500-12,000)
- CARMA and PDJF audience crossover: reach 5,000+ new accounts through partner sharing
- Establish the Armen Zennedjian Classic as an annual event

**Objective 5: Community & Media**
- Secure 1-2 local press placements (Inland Empire-area outlets)
- Generate shareable post-event content that lives beyond June 22
- Build an email list of 500+ engaged contacts for future years

## Target Audience Personas

### Persona 1: "The Inland Empire Business Owner" (Primary Registration Target)
- **Demographics:** 40-65, male-skewing, owns or operates a mid-size business in the IE
- **Motivations:** Networking opportunity, charitable cause with visibility, premium event
- **How to Reach Them:** LinkedIn, email via local chambers, IE business Facebook groups
- **Messaging Angle:** "Spend a day doing something meaningful for the community alongside the people who built it."

### Persona 2: "The Horse Racing & Equestrian Insider" (Highest Emotional Investment)
- **Demographics:** 35-70, mixed gender, connected to California horse racing
- **Motivations:** Honoring Armen's memory, supporting CARMA and PDJF, community reunion
- **How to Reach Them:** CARMA and PDJF channels, racing social communities, Armen's personal network
- **Messaging Angle:** "This day is for Armen — and for every horse and jockey he fought for."

### Persona 3: "The Philanthropic Golfer" (Volume Registration Target)
- **Demographics:** 35-65, regular golfer, charitable but not personally connected to Armen
- **Motivations:** Great course, well-run event, premium experience for the price
- **How to Reach Them:** Golf community social, Morongo's channels, golf hashtags
- **Messaging Angle:** "Play a world-class course for a cause that matters — and leave with a story worth telling."

### Persona 4: "The Corporate Sponsor or Brand Partner" (Highest Revenue Potential)
- **Demographics:** Marketing directors, community relations managers
- **Motivations:** Brand visibility, cause alignment, networking with IE decision-makers
- **How to Reach Them:** LinkedIn/email outreach, chambers, referrals from existing sponsors
- **Messaging Angle:** "Your brand alongside a legacy — and an audience that builds the Inland Empire."

# SECTION 2: SOCIAL MEDIA STRATEGY

## Platform Roles

**Instagram:** Primary visual storytelling platform. Tribute reels, sponsor reveals, countdown content, story polls. Where the emotional depth lives.

**Twitter/X:** Real-time conversation, horse racing community engagement, event-day live updates. Where you find the racing community and build reach.

## Instagram Posting Frequency
- Phase 1-2: 3x/week feed + 2-3 stories/week
- Phase 3: 4x/week feed + daily stories
- Phase 4: Daily feed + 2-3 stories/day
- Event day: Stories throughout, 2-3 feed posts
- Post-event: Daily for 1 week, then 2x/week through July 15

## Instagram Content Pillars
- **Pillar A — Tribute & Legacy (30%):** Armen stories, Derby Room era, community quotes
- **Pillar B — The Causes (25%):** CARMA horse spotlights, PDJF jockey stories, stats
- **Pillar C — Event Experience (20%):** Venue, sponsor spotlights, contests, food teases
- **Pillar D — Countdown & Conversion (15%):** Registration CTAs, urgency, spots remaining
- **Pillar E — Community & Culture (10%):** UGC, horse racing culture, IE community

**Feed posts (3 total):** Morning arrival | Mid-day action | Evening awards

## Core Value Proposition
> "Your brand alongside a room full of decision-makers, a cause people genuinely care about, and a story your audience will remember. You are not buying logo placement at a golf tournament. You are becoming part of Armen Zennedjian's legacy."

**Key stats for pitch:**
- 200+ attendees: IE business owners, community leaders, equestrian professionals
- 10,000+ combined social following
- CARMA + PDJF partner channels: 20,000-40,000+ additional reach
- Inaugural year — first sponsors get best pricing and strongest brand association
- 100% tax-deductible

## Follow-Up Cadence
- Day 0: Initial outreach
- Day 5: Follow-up with one new piece of value (a stat, a course photo)
- Day 12: Social proof angle ("just confirmed [other sponsor]")
- Day 20: Scarcity angle ("X stake-level slots remaining")
- Day 30+: Move to nurture list, invite to follow on social. Keep door open for year 2.
